About me

A short introduction

Hello! I am Guanqiao Feng (凤冠乔), a third-year student at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University's Entrepreneur College (Taicang), studying in the Academy of Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology.

My interests sit at the intersection of networking, distributed computing and machine learning. Through coursework and self-driven projects I have built socket-based streaming applications, MapReduce-style data pipelines, and small machine-learning experiments. I enjoy understanding systems from the packet level up, and I care about measuring things properly instead of guessing.

This site is itself part of a networking assignment: it is deployed with GitHub Pages, then analysed with DNS lookups, ping, traceroute and packet capture.

Projects

Coursework and experiments I have built

Networking

Video streaming over TCP vs UDP

A Python socket application that streams a video file frame by frame over TCP and UDP, with a custom length-prefixed protocol for TCP and fragmentation/reassembly for UDP. Performance is compared with Wireshark captures: throughput, end-to-end latency, packet loss and retransmissions.

  • Python
  • sockets
  • Wireshark
  • tshark

Distributed computing

Word count with MapReduce and Spark

A pure-Python MapReduce implementation (map → shuffle/partition → reduce) compared with Hadoop MapReduce and Apache Spark on the same dataset, focusing on programming effort, execution behaviour and scalability.

  • MapReduce
  • Hadoop
  • Spark

Machine learning

Classification experiments

Supervised learning coursework covering linear and logistic regression, SVM, KNN, kernels and decision trees, with cross-validation and model selection on tabular datasets.
